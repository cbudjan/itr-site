---
title: "International Tea Room"
description: "A monthly virtual seminar series and community for researchers working on embryos and stem cell-based embryo models, particularly gastruloids."
---

The **International Tea Room** is a monthly, virtual seminar series where labs working on
**embryos** and **stem cell-based embryo models (SCBEMs)**, particularly **gastruloids**, share ongoing and
unpublished work, preprints, and the practical details that rarely make it into
conference talks.

It started as informal gatherings in the tea room of the Department of Genetics in
Cambridge, moved online during the pandemic, and has since grown into an international
community. From the 2026–27 season the scope broadens from gastruloids to embryos and
all types of stem cell-based embryo models.
