What happens to axial elongation and patterning when all four Hox clusters are removed:
gastruloids lacking Hox genes still elongate and polarise, but lose definitive endoderm and
fail to form somite-like structures, pointing to TALE-factor complexes as the underlying
machinery that Hox proteins modulate.
