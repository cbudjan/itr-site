Questions about the series, suggestions for speakers, or interest in presenting your own
work? Use the form or email the organizers directly. We usually reply within a week.

### Want to give a talk?

We actively welcome **self-nominations** and suggestions of colleagues. Early-career
researchers presenting preprints or unpublished work are particularly encouraged. Pick
*"Speaker suggestion / I'd like to present"* in the form and tell us briefly what you would
talk about.
